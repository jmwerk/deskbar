Deskbar

A BUSY Bar style status display, focus timer and Jira time tracker for the Spotify Car Thing running bridgething. This demo runs the real app in your browser against a fake device: the Jira issues, the Spotify track and the webhook are all simulated, and nothing leaves the page.
